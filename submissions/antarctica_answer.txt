I think developing resources in Antarctica is worth it because there's a lot of untapped stuff there like oil and minerals that countries could use. The world is running out of resources in other places so getting them from Antarctica makes sense. Also companies could make a lot of money from it which would help the economy.

Some people say it could damage the environment but that's probably not that big of a deal because Antarctica is already really cold and empty so nothing really lives there anyway.

Overall I think we should develop Antarctica because it has lots of resources and money is important for countries.
